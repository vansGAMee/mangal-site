# Browser Sales Research

1. Start only after a working demo URL and verified storefront/order/admin flow exist.
2. Use public official business pages and current listing sources. Confirm the business is open and recently active.
3. Exclude chains, abandoned pages, personal employee profiles, closed venues, and businesses with a strong current ordering site.
4. Record source URLs, observation date, current order method, exact problem, fit reason, activity date, category A/B/C, and contact status.
5. Distinguish observed facts from inference. Do not invent missing details or copy protected images.
6. Deduplicate by official page and business identity.
7. Save 30 qualified rows to `sales/leads.csv`, then prepare the ten best category-A summaries.

Do not send, draft into a live message box, follow, like, or otherwise contact a lead while using this skill.
