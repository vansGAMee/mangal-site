# VK Outreach Human Review

1. Use only an official business recipient and an existing user-authenticated VK session. Never request, view, or store a password.
2. Draft one message under 400 characters using the business name and one verified observation. Avoid promises, pressure, repeated templates, and multiple links.
3. Show recipient, official URL, evidence, reason, final text, and destination before any send action.
4. Require explicit approval for that exact recipient and exact text. Approval never carries to another message.
5. Re-check recipient and text immediately before pressing Send.
6. Send one at a time, no more than five first messages per day. Never bypass rate limits or CAPTCHA.
7. Update `sales/outreach-log.csv` after each action. Stop immediately on mismatch, error, or do-not-contact request.
8. Draft at most one follow-up after 4–7 days, under 250 characters, and require new approval.

Never perform bulk or autonomous outreach.
